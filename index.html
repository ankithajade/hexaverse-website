What is HexaVerse

Six departments.
Six weeks. One experience.

Each week spotlights a different department at Don Bosco Institute of Technology (DBIT), featuring tailored workshops, signature events, and cross-campus mega events that bring every branch together.

🛠️

Hands-on Workshops

Two-day technical workshops each week, designed for the featured department's domain.

🏆

Department Events

Signature competitions and activities curated for each department's strengths.

🎪

Mega Events

Large-scale events every two weeks — open to all departments, no boundaries.

🤝

Cross-Department

Breaking silos. Building a unified campus-wide technical culture together.

Six sides. One story.

The cube is HexaVerse. Each face represents one department — a unique identity, colour, and contribution. Together, all six sides form one complete, unified experience.

A physical cube installation will live on campus throughout the series — collecting signatures, photographs, doodles, QR codes, and memories from every participant.
